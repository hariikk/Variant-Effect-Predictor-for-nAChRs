# Part (f): Author Kit for Publication Venue

## Primary Venue: PSB 2027 (Pacific Symposium on Biocomputing)

### Author Kit / Submission Guidelines

- **Format instructions page:** https://psb.stanford.edu/psb-online/psb-submit/
- **Call for papers:** https://psb.stanford.edu/callfor/papers/
- **Key dates:** https://psb.stanford.edu/keydates/

### Submission Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **File format** | PDF (Adobe Acrobat preferred) |
| **File naming** | Last name of first author (e.g., `krishnamurthy.pdf`) |
| **Page limit** | 12 pages (NOT including cover letter, title page, or references) |
| **Paper size** | US Letter (8.5 x 11 inches) |
| **Color images** | Accepted at no additional charge |
| **Supplemental material** | Referenced by URL (PSB does not host supplemental material) |
| **Cover letter** | Required as first page of submission; must state corresponding author email and target PSB session |

### Key Deadlines (PSB 2027)

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
| Paper submission | August 3, 2026 (11:59 PM PT) |
| Notification of acceptance | September 8, 2026 |
| Camera-ready deadline | October 1, 2026 (11:59 PM PT) |
| Conference | January 3-7, 2027 |

### How to Get the Template

1. Visit https://psb.stanford.edu/psb-online/psb-submit/
2. Download the LaTeX or Word template provided on that page
3. Follow the formatting instructions exactly (font sizes, margins, section headings)

### Proceedings Publication

- PSB proceedings are published by **World Scientific Publishing Company**
- Papers are **indexed in PubMed** (important for visibility in the biomedical community)
- Proceedings are available online at https://psb.stanford.edu/psb-online/

## What You Need to Prepare

1. **Download the author kit** from https://psb.stanford.edu/psb-online/psb-submit/
2. **Identify the target session** — check https://psb.stanford.edu/callfor/papers/ for PSB 2027 session topics (look for sessions on genomic medicine, protein function, computational methods, or machine learning in biology)
3. **Write the cover letter** with your email and chosen session
4. **Format your paper** using the PSB template (12 pages max)
5. **Submit by August 3, 2026**
