## Recommendation

**PSB 2027 (August 3, 2026 deadline)** is the most actionable target. It is a respected venue, the deadline is approaching but reachable, and the paper format (up to 12 pages) allows space to describe the feature engineering, multi-model comparison, and structural feature analysis in detail. The Hawaii setting also provides a good networking opportunity in the biocomputing community.

As a backup, **RECOMB 2027** or **ISMB/ECCB 2027** are higher-profile but more competitive options with later deadlines.
